## Article 4 — Orders, allocation and availability

Orders are placed in writing, by email or through the trade section of the Estate's website.

An order binds the Estate only once it has been confirmed in writing by the Estate. The Estate reserves the right to refuse any order, in particular in the event of an ongoing dispute, a previous payment incident, or incompatibility with its distribution policy.

**Minimum order: 600 bottles.**

The Estate's production comes from limited harvests. Volumes are allocated by cuvée and by vintage. The Estate is under no obligation as to volume from one year to the next, and a previous order creates no right to renewal. Should a cuvée be exhausted after an order has been confirmed, the Estate shall inform the Buyer without delay and refund the corresponding sums, without such unavailability giving rise to any compensation.

The Estate gives no warranty whatsoever as to the development of the wines after loading, the conditions of carriage and storage being entirely outside its control.

The Estate's liability, on any ground whatsoever, is limited to the replacement of bottles acknowledged to be defective or the refund of their invoiced price, to the exclusion of any indirect loss, loss of profit, loss of custom or damage to reputation.
